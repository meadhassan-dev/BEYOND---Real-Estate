Meet the Team

Rafsan Ahmed

Managing Director

Rafsan Ahmed leads the company's strategic direction, project planning, investment vision, and business development. He studied at the Institute of Business Administration (IBA), University of Dhaka, majoring in Finance.

His professional experience spans management consulting, real estate sales, and financial advisory. This background gives him a strong understanding of project feasibility, market positioning, investment structuring, and long-term value creation.

As the Managing Director, he focuses on building a disciplined development company where financial judgment, market insight, construction quality, and client trust work together.

Sayeek Md Majmoon Rahman

Director

Mr. Sayeek leads the company's brand strategy, market positioning, and customer communication. He brings strong experience in building consumer-facing brands, particularly in the hospitality and restaurant sector. He earned his Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree from the University of Rajshahi.

He also owns NorthBurg, a reputed premium dining and bistro brand in Rajshahi. This experience gives him a practical understanding of how strong branding, service quality, atmosphere, and customer trust work together to create market value.

He has also overseen the project management of a six-storey commercial building on his own land, giving him direct exposure to real estate development from an owner's perspective.

Beyond that, he ensures that every project is positioned with clarity, credibility, and emotional appeal. In a market where many buildings often appear similar, his focus is to help each project stand apart through a distinct identity, strong storytelling, and buyer confidence.

Rizvy Md Rizwanur Rahman

Director

Mr. Rizvy oversees client relationship management, buyer communication, after-sales coordination, and the overall customer experience journey. He has around 15 years of experience in client relations across corporate and service-oriented sectors.

His professional background includes experience with Banglalink, logistics company AussieX, and the education consultancy sector. He completed his BBA and MBA from Southeast University.

His strength lies in understanding client expectations, maintaining disciplined communication, resolving concerns, and creating a smooth buyer journey from the first inquiry to final handover.

Akif Hasan Miraj

B.Sc, BUET

Chief Engineer

Miraj leads engineering planning, technical supervision, and construction execution. He studied Civil Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).

He has hands-on experience in managing the end-to-end engineering of a 10-storey residential building on 10 katha land in a prime area of Mirpur DOHS. He has also directly supervised another large residential building project on foundation and ground-floor construction.

His responsibilities at BEYOND include structural coordination, technical supervision, quality monitoring, construction problem-solving, and ensuring that projects are executed according to sound engineering principles. He is also committed to continuous professional learning so that the company can take on increasingly complex and higher-value construction projects with confidence.

Shihab Ahmed

B.Sc, Stamford University

Senior Engineer – Civil & Structural

Mr. Shihab holds a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Civil Engineering from Stamford University Bangladesh and brings over 16 years of hands-on experience in the construction industry. He has extensive experience in both public and private sector projects, providing technical expertise and managerial oversight for medium- to large-scale construction developments.

His practical knowledge of construction processes, combined with his technical and project management capabilities, enables him to effectively oversee complex construction activities.

Md Mahtab Uddin Sujon

Project Manager

Mr. Sujon is an experienced construction professional with extensive hands-on expertise in construction management, project execution, and procurement across both public and private sectors.

He has over 24 years of experience in the construction sector. Beginning his career at an early age, he independently managed the construction of a 6-storey residential building in Banasree, Dhaka, at just age 22. He subsequently worked on multiple building projects for Summit Properties, Shah Islam Construction, Asset Developments & Holdings Ltd, and the construction of a cancer hospital in Mohakhali.

His experience also extends to public infrastructure projects, including school buildings, cyclone shelters, and bridges. With a strong understanding of construction operations, resource management, procurement, and on-site coordination, he brings practical insight and proven project execution capabilities to complex construction developments.

Usman Bin Faiz

Manager, Finance & Accounts

Usman Bin Faiz manages finance, accounting, documentation, reporting support, and internal financial discipline. He has 4.5 years of accounting experience at Mumlook Mushtaque & Co., Chartered Accountants.

He is a CA Professional Level accountant under the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Bangladesh (ICAB).

His role strengthens the company's financial documentation, expense tracking, reporting discipline, taxation and internal audit control practices.

Aysha Islam

Senior Executive, Sales

Ms. Aysha Islam supports sales operations, client communication, local buyer engagement, and field-level sales coordination. She has practical experience in the Rajshahi real estate market, giving her strong familiarity with local buyer expectations, location preferences, pricing sensitivity, and project-level demand patterns.

Her role is especially important in maintaining direct communication with prospective buyers, understanding market feedback, and supporting a smooth sales process.

Orin Sharmin Sultana

Legal Counsel

Ms. Orin supports the company on land due diligence, legal documentation, developer agreements, buyer agreements, compliance, and regulatory matters. She is a practicing advocate and a member of both the Supreme Court Bar Association and the Rajshahi Advocate Bar Association. She has also served as a Lecturer in Law at a university. In addition to her legal practice, she serves as a panel lawyer for several reputed banks in Rajshahi and is a member of the Rajshahi Tax Lawyers Association.

She has a strong practical understanding of land and real estate law, with particular experience in the Rajshahi region. Her role is central to protecting the interests of landowners, buyers, investors, and the company through proper legal review, documentation, and risk management. Her advisory support helps ensure that projects are structured with transparency and legal discipline from the very beginning.
